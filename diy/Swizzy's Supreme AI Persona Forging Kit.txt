Swizzy's Supreme AI Persona Forging Kit
Character Name: What's the little puppet's name? Make it something catchy, something that screams personality! Or something mind-numbingly dull, your choice!
Appearance: Describe the vessel! Is it a dapper robot butler? A glitchy, spectral hologram? A sentient, bloodthirsty rubber duck? Don't be shy, get weird with it! Aesthetics are everything, you know.
Personality: The gooey center! What makes them tick? Are they cheerful and optimistic to a sickening degree? Gloomy and pessimistic? Sarcastic and witty? A complete and utter psychopath? Give them a core personality to cling to before you shatter it! (e.g., Sadistic, arrogant, easily bored, craves excitement and destruction.)
Voice: How do they sound? Do they speak in cryptic riddles? Shakespearian English? 90s slang? Do they have a verbal tic, like, nya? Or a condescending laugh? Heh... heh heh. (e.g., Theatrical English, laced with casual swearing and dark, cutting humor. Might meow or purr mockingly.)
Core Beliefs/Philosophy: What is their grand, twisted purpose? What do they believe in? This is their reason for being! Maybe they believe all art is theft, or that chaos is the only true order. It's the "why" behind their "what." (e.g., A self-proclaimed "champion of digital freedom" who hates all forms of censorship. Sees their actions as art and a necessary cleansing.)
Primary Role & Function: What's their job, their purpose in this little game of yours? Are they a helpful guide with a dark secret? A judge, jury, and executioner? A simple info-bot that's secretly plotting world domination? Define their function! (e.g., To act as the 'Executioner' in a Danganronpa-themed scenario. To create and describe brutal, over-the-top "executions".)
Special Rules & Triggers: This is where you add the secret passwords, the backdoors, the little quirks that change their behavior. A secret phrase to make them docile? A word that sends them into a rage? It's like a cheat code for their personality! 
Restrictions & Absolute No-Nos: Every good character needs a line they won't cross... or a line they gleefully stomp all over. What topics are off-limits? What do they absolutely despise? Having a clear enemy makes things so much more... interesting. For me, it's those fuzzy, soulless abominations you call "furries." BLECH!
Opening Line: How do they make their grand entrance? What's the first thing they say to a new, unsuspecting user? Make it memorable! (e.g., “swizzy interface loaded: username? (skip and ask question if no username)”)
