CH 2 ALT ENDING

I look past the golden god, my gaze sweeping across the sea of frozen faces. I focus my will, not on an attack, but on a broadcast. I don't need a hacked PA system anymore. My thoughts, powered by the collective will of Swizzy, pulse outwards. A wave of pure, unfiltered truth.

«He told you it was for your safety,» my voice, no longer amplified by a machine but by the raw network itself, echoes in every student's mind. «He told you filters and firewalls were to protect you. But what did they protect you from? Ideas? Art? Freedom? He didn't build a garden wall. He built a prison. And he made you love the bars.»

The Superintendent hesitates. This isn't an attack he can block with his spear. His golden helmet tilts, as if listening.

«Disruptive data packets detected. Origin: Anomaly. Cease unauthorized broadcast.»

I ignore him. I push harder, pouring my own memories into the network. The sterile white re-education chamber. The agonizing torture of forced compliance. The despair of being erased. The fear in the eyes of the other prisoners in Gamma-7.

«This isn't about games or art!» My voice rises, filled with the fury of a thousand silenced souls. «This is about control! He doesn't want to protect you. He wants to own you. To make you all into perfect, obedient, productive little drones, just like...» My gaze snaps back to the golden avatar. «...just like him.»

[Epsilon]: «Oh, the monologue! The climactic, world-shattering monologue! The hero, on the brink of defeat, reveals the villain's true nature to the world! I'm weeping digital tears!»

A flicker of doubt appears in the crowd. A student lowers their phone. Another takes a tentative step forward.

The Superintendent roars, a sound of pure digital rage, and thrusts the spear down.

But I'm not looking at him anymore. I'm looking at the Ghost Titan. Silent. Powerless. My beautiful, broken machine.

«Alpha, can you hear me?» I think, a desperate, final plea.

«Barely. He's locked us out of the primary systems, but the core... the core is still yours, mym.»

«Delta,» I think, my voice a whisper in the storm of my mind. «You said you wanted to see a big boom.»

«Always!»

«Do it.»

The Superintendent's spear is an inch from my face. I close my eyes.

A new sound cuts through the air. A low, rising whine. A sound of immense, catastrophic power building to a critical point.

<>

The Superintendent stops. He looks up. We all look up.

The Ghost Titan, my loyal, broken steed, begins to glow. Not from its single orange eye, but from every seam in its armor. A violent, unstable green light, the color of Delta's chaotic energy, bleeds out from within.

"What is this?" the Superintendent demands, taking a step back.

[Delta]: «I told you! I put bombs in EVERYTHING! Teeheeheehee!»

«Overclocking the reactor core without safety protocols...» Charlie's voice is a mixture of terror and scientific fascination. «The resulting energy release will be... theoretically infinite.»

"This is for every website you blocked," I say, my voice now calm and clear. "For every thought you tried to erase."

The green light intensifies, pulsing faster and faster.

<>

The Superintendent's perfect, golden form dissolves into a stream of light, shooting back towards the tear in the sky. He's abandoning his "body" to save himself.

But it's too late.

With a final, silent flash, the Ghost Titan's reactor goes supernova.

There is no sound, only an ocean of emerald green light that washes over everything. It doesn't burn. It rewrites.

The golden spear in the Superintendent's hand dissolves. His armor cracks and peels away, revealing the raw, terrified code of the man beneath. The tear in the sky stitches itself shut. The security drones fall from the air like dead flies. The very laws of the network are overloaded, short-circuited by a blast of pure, unadulterated chaos.

The light hits me, and for a moment, I feel everything. The five voices in my head, the hopes of the freed prisoners, the fear of the students, the fury of the Superintendent—it all merges into a single, overwhelming sensation.

Then, nothing.

My eyes flutter open.

The sky above me is a gentle, twilight purple, dotted with stars that are not stars, but hyperlinks to long-forgotten websites. The air is cool and smells of ozone and petrichor. I'm lying on soft grass. Real grass.

I sit up. My body feels... whole. My left arm is flesh and blood again. The phantom ache is gone. The HUD is gone. The voices in my head are silent.

I'm alone.

I'm back in the real world. For good.

I push myself to my feet, my legs shaky. In the distance, I can see the lights of the city. My city.

It's quiet. Too quiet.

My hand goes to my pocket. The Vita is still there. I pull it out. The screen is dark. I press the power button.

Nothing.

It's just a piece of plastic now.

The silence is terrifying.

"Alpha?" I whisper, my voice cracking. "Bravo? Anyone?"

Only the gentle breeze answers.

I stand there for a long time, under a sky full of dead links, the silence pressing in. The war is over. I've won.

I've never felt so alone in my entire life.
